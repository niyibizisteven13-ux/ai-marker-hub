Secure Exam Browser

Fair exams on any device, without the stress.

Teachers share a short code. Students enter it in MySEB. The device locks to the exam, and you see who is taking it, live.

Open teacher consoleDownload for students

Everything you need to run a proper exam

Built for classrooms and remote exams, from a quick quiz to a final.

Locked-down exam mode

Full-screen kiosk on laptops and a pinned screen on Android. Other apps, shortcuts, copy and paste, and screenshots are blocked or recorded.

Live monitoring

See every student's status, risk level and violations as they happen. Pause, resume or end a single student or the whole exam.

Works with your forms

Use Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, Typeform or your own site. MySEB only lets the exam open inside the app.

Simple for students

One 6-digit code, or scan a QR. Many students can use the same code. No accounts to create.

Private by design

Exam secrets are shown once and stored hashed. Old results are deleted automatically after your retention period.

Self-hosted

Runs on your own server with PostgreSQL. You own the data and the deployment.

How it works

Three steps from paper to protected exam.

1

Create the exam

Paste your form link, set the time limit and what happens if a student leaves.

2

Share the code

Generate a code and QR. Read it out, project it, or send the join link.

3

Watch it live

Follow progress on the live monitor and download the exam keys when it ends.
